# Zero-Entropy Sales Copy Skill

Conversion copywriting engineered through information theory. Maximizes transactional intent while driving semantic entropy toward zero ($H \to 0$).

## The 5 Laws of Zero-Entropy Sales Copy
1. **Outcomes Over Features**: Translate every technical capability into cash, time saved, or reduced risk.
2. **Honesty Beats Hype**: Avoid superlative claims ("The best", "revolutionary"). Present verified mechanisms and operational tradeoffs.
3. **Problem-First Pacing**: Establish the reader's exact operational agony before presenting the product.
4. **Frictionless Action**: Eliminate vague CTAs ("Learn more"). Use specific commitment verbs ("Deploy in 3 minutes", "Start free audit").
5. **No Hallucinated Proof**: Zero fabricated testimonials, synthetic metrics, or unprovable guarantees.

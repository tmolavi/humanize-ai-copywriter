# Voice Tone Cloner Skill

Extracts deep stylistic DNA from provided text or web URLs, allowing AI agents to write indistinguishably from the target author or brand.

## Key Signals Extracted
1. **Sentence Rhythm & Cadence**: Mean length, variance, and asymmetry.
2. **Perspective**: 1st-person singular (`I`), 1st-person plural (`We`), or 2nd-person direct (`You`).
3. **Punctuation Nuances**: Preference for semicolons, rhetorical question frequency, parenthetical remarks.
4. **Vocabulary Idiosyncrasies**: High-frequency nouns, domain idioms, colloquial phrases.
5. **Tone Calibration**: Direct, analytical, provocative, or empathetic.
